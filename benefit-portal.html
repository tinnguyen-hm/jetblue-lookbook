Clinical-Grade Custom Insoles

Exclusive Crewmember Pricing from Hike Medical
Eligible
Benefit Summary

Hike Medical delivers custom insoles built specifically for your unique feet. As a JetBlue crewmember, you have access to Hike custom insoles at an exclusive rate, no clinic visits, no doctor's referral, and no insurance paperwork.

When your feet aren't supported, your knees absorb extra stress, your hips compensate, and your lower back tightens to pick up the slack. The fatigue and soreness follows you the rest of the day, affecting everything from your mood to your sleep. Custom insoles are built to fit your actual foot shape, helping your body move the way it's meant to, instead of offloading the work up your skeleton.

Your Pricing
Standard rate: $150 per pair.
Reorder rate: $135 per pair on any pair after your first, a $15 discount.
Family rate: $135 per pair for a family member, at the same discounted rate, unlocked through a special access link after you order your first pair. Contact Hike support at support@hikemedical.com to request access.
How It Works
1Smart Value: JetBlue crewmembers get exclusive pricing on Hike custom insoles at $150 per pair, clinical-grade support without the clinic costs or wait.
2Simple Process: Complete a 5-minute scanning process from your phone and receive your custom pair, designed for your unique foot shape, in about 2 weeks.
3Fit Guarantee: Amazing fit guarantee, or we rebuild them for free.
4Keep Saving: Reorder any time at $135 a pair, and share the same $135 rate with a family member through a special access link.

FAQ

Is this covered by insurance?No. This is a voluntary benefit paid directly to Hike at your exclusive JetBlue rate, not an insurance claim. There's no paperwork or insurance involved.

Can I order more than one pair?Yes. Your first pair is $150. Any pair after that is $135, a $15 savings.

Can I share this with a family member?Yes. Contact Hike support at support@hikemedical.com for a special access link that extends the $135 rate to a family member.

Who is eligible?All JetBlue crewmembers.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?No prescription required. Order anytime, no appointments needed.

What if they don't fit?Amazing fit guarantee. If your insoles aren't fitting right, we'll rebuild them for free.

Why do you need my personal information?We check your details against the eligible crewmember list JetBlue shared with us to confirm you're covered.

Is my information private during the scan?Yes. The scan captures only the shape of your feet. No location data is recorded.

What if I need help?Support is available by text, email, in-portal chat, and phone. Hike User Support: support@hikemedical.com | 309-306-3696.

Eligibility to receive Hike Custom Insoles is subject to the agreement between your organization and Hike Medical, including your current employment and/or membership status. Questions? Contact your benefits administrator or support@hikemedical.com.
