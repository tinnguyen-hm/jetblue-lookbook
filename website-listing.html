Custom Insoles

Exclusive Crewmember Pricing from Hike Medical

Drugstore inserts are molded once, for an average foot, and sold to millions of different feet. They flatten out fast, especially on the miles crewmembers put in on ramps, jet bridges, and aisles. Getting truly custom insoles, designed to your exact foot geometry, through a specialist means spending $500 to $1,000 and waiting months. Hike closes that gap, delivering the same clinical-grade custom insoles at an exclusive JetBlue rate, without the wait.

Your Pricing

JetBlue crewmembers have access to Hike custom insoles at $150 per pair, an exclusive rate paid directly to Hike, no claims or paperwork required.

Reorder any pair after your first at $135, a $15 discount. Want to share the benefit? Extend the same discounted rate to a family member through a special access link, unlocked after you order your first pair. Contact Hike support at support@hikemedical.com to request access.

Get Started In Person

Hike will be onsite at select JetBlue Benefits Fairs throughout October to help crewmembers scan and order in person.

Eligibility to receive Hike Custom Insoles is subject to the agreement between your organization and Hike Medical, including your current employment and/or membership status. Questions? Contact your benefits administrator or support@hikemedical.com.
